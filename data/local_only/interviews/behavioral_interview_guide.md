# Behavioral Interview Guide — Senior AI Engineer

## Opening
Thank the candidate, set agenda: 45-minute technical + behavioral session.
Remind them we value clear thinking over perfect answers.

## Question: Ownership Under Pressure
Tell me about a time a model you owned failed in production.
What happened, what was your role, and what did you change afterward?

## Question: Cross-Team Collaboration
Describe a project where you had to align with a team that didn't share your
technical priorities. How did you handle the disagreement?

## Question: Scope Management
Walk me through a project where the scope changed significantly after kickoff.
How did you re-plan and communicate the impact to stakeholders?

## Talking Point: Career Motivation
Ask what draws the candidate to platform work specifically — not just AI.
Strong candidates articulate why building the system matters, not just
building the model.

## Red Flag: Blame Externalization
Watch for candidates who attribute all failures to management, timelines, or
tooling without reflecting on their own role or what they would change.

## Concern: Depth vs. Breadth Trade-off
Some strong candidates have broad skills but shallow depth in production systems.
Probe with a follow-up: "What's the hardest production debugging session you've had?"
