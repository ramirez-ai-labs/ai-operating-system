# Role Brief  -  Senior AI Engineer, Platform Team

## Role Overview
The Senior AI Engineer owns the design and delivery of production AI capabilities on the
platform team. This role sits at the intersection of ML engineering and platform engineering  - 
candidates must be comfortable building reliable systems, not just models.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and operate model serving infrastructure for sub-100ms inference
- Build and maintain evaluation pipelines for production LLM workflows
- Partner with product and data teams to scope and deliver AI features
- Participate in on-call rotation for AI-adjacent production incidents

## Must-Have Qualifications
- Experience taking ML models to production (not just prototyping)
- Comfort with observability tooling (traces, metrics, alerts for model quality)
- Strong Python and at least one major ML framework

## Nice-to-Have
- LLM application development (RAG, tool use, agents)
- MCP or similar protocol familiarity
- Experience in regulated industries (fintech, healthcare)

## Question: Production Ownership
Discuss: Walk me through the last model you owned end-to-end in production.
What did "ownership" mean  -  did you own the SLOs, the on-call, the rollback?

## Talking Point: Evaluation Culture
Our team runs eval pipelines on every PR. Candidates should be able to discuss
what good model evaluation looks like and how they've done it before.

## Red Flag: Model-Only Focus
Candidates who can only talk about model architecture and not system design,
reliability, or operational concerns are not a fit for this role.
